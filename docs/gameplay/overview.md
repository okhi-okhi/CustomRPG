### **Goals**

* **Explore the world:** Players will travel to many different locations, each with its own unique challenges and rewards.
* **Gather powerful equipment:** Players will find and equip powerful weapons, armor, and artifacts to boost their strength and abilities.
* **Recruit new allies:** Players will meet a variety of characters who can join their team, each with their own unique skills and abilities.
* **Build your reputation:** Players' choices will affect their reputation, which will in turn affect how other characters interact with them.

### **Mechanics**

* [x] **Team System:** Players can choose up to four characters to form their team. Each character has their own unique abilities and skills, so players must carefully consider their team composition to create a balanced and effective team.
* [ ] **Forge System:** Players can use materials they collect from enemies and quests to forge new equipment. Equipment can provide characters with stat bonuses, special abilities, or even new skills.
* [x] **Reputation System:** Players' reputations will affect how they are treated by non-player characters (NPCs). A good reputation can lead to discounts from merchants, while a bad reputation can make it difficult to find allies.
* [x] **Equipment System:** Each character can equip weapons, armor, and other accessories.
* [ ] **Merchant System:** Players can trade with merchants to buy and sell equipment, items, and information. However, some merchants may be dishonest or even dangerous.
* [x] **Random Events:** Players will encounter random events throughout the game. These events can be both positive and negative, and they can have a significant impact on the game's outcome.
* [x] **Character System:** Each character has a race and a class. These factors will affect the character's stats, abilities, and skills.
* [ ] **Battle System:** Battles are turn-based, with each character taking turns to attack or cast spells. Players must carefully consider their actions to defeat their enemies.
* [ ] **Skill System:** Each character has a variety of skills that they can use in battle. These skills can be used to deal damage, heal allies, or even control the battlefield.
* [ ] **Gacha System:** Players can use in-game currency to purchase loot boxes that contain new characters and equipment.
* [ ] **Upgrade System:** Equipment and skills can be upgraded to improve their effectiveness. However, this requires resources that can be difficult to obtain.
* [x] **Location System:** The game world is made up of a variety of places, each with its own unique challenges and rewards.
